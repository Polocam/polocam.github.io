Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Future Signal is a personal tool used by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services with the single permission to upload videos.

Data we access: only the owner's own Google account, with the owner's permission, and only to upload videos to the owner's channel.

Data we collect, store, share or sell about other people: none. The application has no other users and does not collect personal data from visitors.

By using YouTube API Services, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/Polocam/future-signal/issues.
